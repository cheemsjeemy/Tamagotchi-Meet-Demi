- Demi stats:
  - Hunger: Decreases over time, feed Demi to restore.
  - Happiness: Increases with interaction, decreases if ignored.
  - Energy: Decreases over time, replenished by letting Demi sleep.
  - Health: Affected by hunger and energy levels, decreases if neglected.
  - Cleanliness: Decreases over time, clean Demi to restore.

- Activities:
  - Feed Demi: Restores hunger and slightly increases happiness.
  - Play with Demi: Increases happiness but decreases energy.
  - Let Demi Sleep: Restores energy but decreases hunger slightly.
  - Clean Demi: Restores cleanliness and slightly increases happiness.

- Random Events:
  - Demi gets sick if hunger or energy is too low.
  - Demi asks for attention (e.g., "Play with me!").
  - Demi finds a snack, slightly restoring hunger.
  - Demi evolves or changes appearance based on stats.

- Visual Feedback:
  - Happy Sprite: When happiness is high.
  - Sad Sprite: When happiness is low.
  - Sleeping Sprite: When Demi is sleeping.
  - Sick Sprite: When Demi is unwell.
  - Dirty Sprite: When cleanliness is low.

- Sound Effects:
  - Happy beep when Demi is fed or played with.
  - Sad beep when Demi is ignored or stats are low.
  - Sleep sound when Demi goes to sleep.

- Mini-Games:
  - Catch the falling object: Use touch sensors to catch objects.
  - Memory game: Repeat a sequence of beeps Demi plays.

- Dynamic Stats Updates:
  - Stats decrease over time (e.g., hunger, energy, cleanliness).
  - Random events trigger occasionally to keep the experience engaging.
